Taste exemplar picker

Open this file straight from disk; every video host played from a file in Chrome (checked 2026-10-16). If a video ever stays blank, serve the folder instead: from the repo root run python -m http.server 8123 --directory banana-server/bench/taste and open http://localhost:8123/picker.html.

Pick the pictures that best show your bar (docs/taste-bar.md), separately for each kind of work: aim for 20 to 40 per tab. They become the exemplars the Atlas judge compares results against for that kind of work. Click a picture, or Tab to it and press Space or Enter, to pick or unpick it. Hover or focus a picture to play its video or run through its frames. Press "View all" under a picture to open every frame and video large: Left and Right move between them and Esc closes; opening it never changes a pick. Switch tabs with the arrow keys. Your saved picks start checked; tabs you have not picked yet start with my defaults. "Load my picks.json" reopens a file you saved, so you can add to it. When you are done, press Download and save the file as banana-server/bench/taste/picks.json. Pictures and videos are hotlinked; each one links to its source page.
