Taste exemplar picker

Pick the 20 to 40 pictures that best show your bar (docs/taste-bar.md). They become the exemplars the Atlas judge compares results against. Click a picture, or Tab to it and press Space or Enter, to pick or unpick it. 40 are picked to start: 30 from your boards and 10 search finds. When you are done, press Download and save the file as banana-server/bench/taste/picks.json. Pictures are hotlinked; each one links to its source page.
