---
title: Your taste profile
description: A few style words that break ties when GoBanana bends a brief. How to fill it from your own images, how it is used, and how to clear it.
---

Your taste profile is a short list of style words, such as "minimal" or "dark mode". GoBanana keeps it for your account and uses it in one small way: when you bend a brief and neither your request nor the canvas gives any taste words, the profile words steer the taste search. It never changes how well an idea fits your brief.

## Fill it from your own images

1. Open **Account** and go to **Taste profile**.
2. Choose **Choose images** and pick 5 to 20 of your favourite images.
3. GoBanana saves them as your uploads (a Pro feature, with the normal size limit and storage quota), reads their colors for a moment, and keeps only the style words they share.

Use PNG or JPEG. At least 3 of your images must be readable.

## What is kept

Words only. The profile never holds images. If Pinterest import is switched on for your account, it reads your own boards once with your consent and keeps style words and links to the pins.

## Clear it

In **Account**, **Taste profile**, choose **Clear**. Deleting your account removes it too.
