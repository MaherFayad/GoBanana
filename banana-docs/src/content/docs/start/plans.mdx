## Always free

On every plan, with no quota:

- `bend_brief`: bending a brief into directions, as often as you like
- `rate_references` and `rate_directions`: teaching search what to keep
- `get_canvas`, `add_to_canvas`, `arrange_canvas`, `open_canvas`, `new_canvas` (within the canvas and item limits)
- Frankenstein it: `cut_parts`, `frankenstein_page`, `remix_frankenstein`, `update_frankenstein`
- `extract_palette` with WCAG contrast
- `delete_canvas`

Only `search_references` counts against the monthly search quota: one call is one search, whatever it returns.

## What happens at a limit

The tool answers with one plain message and an upgrade link, for example:

```text
You have used your 30 free searches this month. Upgrade: https://gobanana-server.maherfayad55.workers.dev/en/pricing
```

The skills show that message once and stop. They never retry in a loop. At the canvas limit they offer two ways out: add to one of your boards, or delete an old one (only after you say yes to a named board).

Full numbers, every message and the reset rules: [Plans and limits reference](/docs/reference/plans/).
