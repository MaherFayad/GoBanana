# Illustrative and handmade (`illustrative-handmade`)

Part of the GoBanana style index (docs/styles/INDEX.md). The general skill that reads a style from a
thumbnail is `gobanana-taste` in banana-plugin. Load this file when a direction names `illustrative-handmade`.

## Definition

Drawn, printed or collaged artwork carries the identity.

## Visual signals

Check these in the thumbnail. Three or more yes means the reference is this style.

- A visible line or brush.
- A limited ink palette.
- Flat figures with texture, or botanical and folk drawing.
- A collage of cut shapes.

Genres: Brand, Web showcase, Mobile, Deck.

## Great versus cheap (the owner's bar, docs/taste-bar.md)

- **Great:** A recognisable hand and a system: one line weight, one palette.
- **Cheap:** Flat vector stock people, Corporate Memphis, AI illustration with six-fingered hands.

## Do

- Commission or credit one hand; keep its line weight.
- Use a limited palette shared with the UI.
- Let illustration carry one moment, not every screen.

## Don't

- No stock vector people.
- No AI-made illustration presented as craft.
- No mixing three illustration styles.

## Nearest neighbour: `playful-too-fun` (Playful, too fun)

Look at the feel. Illustrative can be calm, quiet or literary; playful is loud and energetic.

## Judge rubric

Use it in rate_references, the Bench judge and critique. A reference is rated against the brief's
page first, then against this style.

1. **Is it this style?** Yes when 3 or more signals above show in the thumbnail. If it is closer to
   `playful-too-fun`, tag it that way instead.
2. **great:** on the brief's page, could sit on the owner's boards, one focal idea, real content,
   and none of the cheap marks.
3. **good:** on style and on page, but generic.
4. **bad:** off page, off style when the style was asked, or any cheap mark: Flat vector stock people, Corporate Memphis, AI illustration with six-fingered hands.
5. **Slop flags (J8 default AI look), each lowers the grade one step:** a centred hero over three
   rounded 12 to 29 px cards, a soft indigo or violet gradient, system-ui plus a Georgia accent,
   gradient text.

## Lens notes

Sadu motifs drawn by hand, botanical Najdi doors and Qatt Asiri wall painting are strong; credit the craft and its region.
